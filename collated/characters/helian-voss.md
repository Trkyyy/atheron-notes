# Magister Helian Voss

## Summary
Scholar Ward researcher focused on pre-veil artifacts and chaos trends. Later confirmed dead under suspicious circumstances that conflict with official reporting.

## Known Facts
- At the Frostviel Gala, Voss discussed rising chaos energy and risks of chaos storms. [Source: Sessions/5. Frostviel Gala.md]
- Voss identified The Matrix as puzzle-class pre-veil technology and discussed artifact uncertainty. [Source: Sessions/5.5. Meeting with the Crimson Cartel.md]
