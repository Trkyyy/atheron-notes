# Soren

## Summary
Mysterious monk-like ally encountered on Vaelor's Wound who claims to have been stranded on-island for centuries and later joins key operations.

## Aliases
- Serun (spelling variant)

- Soren appears during a nighttime bear attack and introduces himself with a mastiff named Gorr. [Source: Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
- He states knowledge of local ruins and identifies the AOI/palace context. [Source: Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
- After return to Corsava, Soren reports he has been on the island for 225 years. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
