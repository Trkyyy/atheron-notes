# Aurelian Frostmark (The Winter Regent)

## Summary
Masked leader tied to repeated crises in Corsava, the Frostviel Gala takeover, and a later confrontation in a frozen realm. Evidence suggests "Aurelian Frostmark" may be a role or mantle linked to a dangerous mask rather than one continuous individual.

## Aliases
- The Winter Regent
- Aurelian Frostmark
- "guy wearing the mask" (party shorthand)

## Known Facts
- A figure identified as The Winter Regent led the Frostviel Gala hostage operation and theft of The Matrix. [Source: Sessions/5. Frostviel Gala.md - "Captain Arelian Frostmark (The Winter Regant) is the boss" and vault item "This is the matrix"]
- A body in the vault, associated with prior Winter Regent history, answered "For a time" when asked if it was Aurelian Frostmark. [Source: Sessions/5.5. Meeting with the Crimson Cartel.md - Speak with Dead responses]
- The same corpse answered "No" to whether Aurelian Frostmark is a self-bestowed title and answered "The mask" when asked why they were Aurelian Frostmark. [Source: Sessions/5.5. Meeting with the Crimson Cartel.md]
- The party later fought and killed a masked Winter Regent manifestation in a frozen realm, after which the body deflated when the mask was removed. [Source: Sessions/12. Getting bling.md; Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]
- Journal fragments in Winter Regent chambers describe the mask as empowering but mind-eroding. [Source: Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md - fragments about "The mask... allows me to do things" and "I can’t even remember my own face"]
- Historical notes connect Winter Regent appearances across long intervals (roughly 50-100+ year gaps), including apparent prior death. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md - history bullets]

## Relationships
- Shadow: receives instructions/correspondence and appears subordinate to Shadow's broader plan. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md letters addressed to Shadow; Sessions/13... letters]
- Caldrake: correspondence references Caldrake needing The Matrix and potentially acting in parallel. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md; Sessions/13... parchment/letters]
- Lightbringers: identified as an obstacle by Shadow-aligned planning. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md - "return of the Lightbringers... could get in our way"]
- Corsava/Auric leadership: targeted during gala operation and wider destabilization efforts. [Source: Sessions/5. Frostviel Gala.md]

## Timeline Mentions

- Session 12-13: Direct encounter in frozen realm; masked form defeated; chamber of five thrones discovered.
