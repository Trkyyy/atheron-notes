# Shadow

## Summary
High-level planner and probable member of an ancient power structure connected to "Monarch" and the five thrones. Operates through correspondents and servants rather than direct appearances.

## Aliases
- Shadow

## Known Facts
- Winter Regent correspondence repeatedly reports to Shadow and references long-term operations in Corsava. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md letters; Sessions/13... letters]
- A parchment recovered after defeating a masked Winter Regent directs action "before they discover what Caldrake is working on with the Matrix - Shadow." [Source: Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]
- A servant in the obsidian structure identifies itself as "Number 3" and says it was created by Shadow to destroy Lightbringers. [Source: Sessions/10. Into the mountains.md]
- Lady Crimson states Shadow is likely her brother Noir, whom she has not spoken to in about 200 years, but this is not independently confirmed. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]

## Relationships
- The Winter Regent: operational reporting line and strategic direction. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md; Sessions/13...]
- Caldrake: linked via Matrix-related plans. [Source: Sessions/13... parchment]
- Lightbringers: treated as adversaries. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md; Sessions/10...]
- Lady Crimson / Noir: possible identity link (Unconfirmed). [Source: Sessions/14...]

## Timeline Mentions
- Session 10: Shadow-linked servant ("Number 3") defeated.
- Session 13: Shadow-signed parchment and letters recovered.
- Session 14: Lady Crimson speculation about Shadow/Noir identity.

## Open Questions
