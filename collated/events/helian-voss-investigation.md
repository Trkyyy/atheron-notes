# Helian Voss Death Investigation

## Summary
Post-expedition inquiry into the death of Magister Helian Voss uncovered conflicting official narratives, hidden research, and investigative links connecting disappearances, chaos experimentation, and major trade actors.

## Aliases
- Voss case

## Known Facts
- On return to Corsava, party was told Voss died one month earlier in a "magic experiment gone wrong." [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
- Scene evidence showed blast/crystal growth patterns and hidden lab material on chaos stone transfer experiments. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
- Hidden notes explicitly referenced experiments based on Caldrake's rejected work. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
- Lera reported suspicious hooded figures near lecture hall and Voss's body condition inconsistent with simple accident. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
- Crypt inspection found jaw removal, preventing Speak with Dead questioning. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
