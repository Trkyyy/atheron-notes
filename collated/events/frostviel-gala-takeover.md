# Frostviel Gala Takeover

## Summary
Coordinated hostage event during the Frostviel Gala in Corsava, culminating in a violent assault, revelation of Winter Regent command, and theft of The Matrix from vault shelf 374.

- Parallel operation powered a "Frostviel machine" and moved hostages, including Elsa Corsava. [Source: Sessions/5. Frostviel Gala.md]
