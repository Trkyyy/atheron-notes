# Vaelor's Wound Expedition

## Summary
Consortium-backed expedition to chaos-saturated island ruins seeking pre-veil leads; involved ship combat, major losses, anomalous locations, and discovery of palace pathways toward Winter Regent and Monarch-linked lore.

## Aliases
- AOI expedition
- Island expedition

## Known Facts
- Jakobus Ket identified Vaelor's Wound as high-chaos, failed-colony territory with prior artifact finds. [Source: Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md]
- Expedition leadership included Adrian Nebbs, Captain Vrosh, and academy scholars. [Source: Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md]
- En route conflict included pirate engagement and later kraken attack causing ship loss and commandeering of pirate vessel. [Source: Sessions/8. Rough Seas.md]
- Ground exploration found ruins, chaos-corrupted fauna, the transient "Last Word" tavern encounter, and a preserved palace complex. [Source: Sessions/8. Rough Seas.md; Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
- Expedition path ultimately led to mountain structures, Shadow-linked entities, and eventual route to Winter Regent domain access. [Source: Sessions/10-13]
