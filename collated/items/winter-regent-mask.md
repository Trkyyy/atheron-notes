# Winter Regent Mask

## Summary
Identity-transforming and likely corruptive artifact that appears to confer power while eroding personhood, strongly tied to the Winter Regent mantle.

## Aliases
- White mask
- Silvery white mask

## Known Facts
- Character notes describe Winter Regent's distinctive full-face white mask and ancient persistence theme. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md]
- Speak with Dead responses indicate the mask is causally linked to becoming Aurelian Frostmark. [Source: Sessions/5.5. Meeting with the Crimson Cartel.md]
- During session 12/13 removal, mask emits extreme cold, triggers a throne vision, and leaves behind a deflating host body. [Source: Sessions/12. Getting bling.md; Sessions/13...]
- Journal fragments describe initial boon followed by cognitive degradation in wearer. [Source: Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]
