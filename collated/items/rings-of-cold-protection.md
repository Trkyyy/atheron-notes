# Rings of Cold Protection

## Summary
Specialized rings forged by the cyclops blacksmith using Chaos Stone and sun-blessed rocks, enabling survivability in Winter Regent conditions.

## Aliases
- Rings of protection from the cold

## Known Facts
- Blacksmith explicitly requested Chaos Stone and rocks blessed by Sun Goddess to craft anti-cold rings. [Source: Sessions/11. Mining for the Cyclops.md]
- Rings grant resistance to cold while charged and can negate cold damage with charge expenditure; a third effect remains unknown. [Source: Sessions/12. Getting bling.md]
- Acquisition directly preceded forced transition into Winter Regent's frozen realm, validating immediate strategic relevance. [Source: Sessions/12. Getting bling.md]

## Relationships

- Winter realm arc: required protective gear.
