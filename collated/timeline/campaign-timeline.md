- Party explores obsidian structure with shattered-crown motifs.

- Cyclops blacksmith identifies material requirements for cold-protection rings.

## Session 12 - Getting bling
- Rings forged; immediate shift into Winter realm where masked Winter Regent confronts party.

## Session 13 - We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!
- Extended Winter realm exploration reveals five thrones and Shadow correspondence.

- Return reveals 4-month timeskip relative to party perspective.

- Hidden lab links disappearances, chaos crystal experimentation, Golden Sails activity, and Caldrake threads.

- Party begins downtime investigations into missing persons. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
