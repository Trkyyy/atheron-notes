# The Last Word

## Summary
Anomalous tavern-like location that appears/disappears and serves as a meeting point for a messenger linked to the Twelve.

## Aliases
- "The last word"

## Known Facts
- Party encountered a cobblestone building with magically lit lantern and sign reading "The last word" in island clearing. [Source: Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
- Occupant in black suit called "Names" delivered strategic warning from the Twelve regarding Winter pursuit. [Source: Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
- The location was described by the occupant as a place that "comes and goes." [Source: Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
