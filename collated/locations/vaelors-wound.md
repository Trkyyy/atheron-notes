# Vaelor's Wound

## Summary
Chaos-heavy island with failed colonial history, pre-veil traces, dangerous fauna, and palace ruins central to current campaign escalation.

## Aliases
- AOI (area of interest)

## Known Facts
- Described as long-uninhabited with repeated failed colonization due to high chaos levels. [Source: Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md]
- Local reports and encounters include giant lizards, corrupted bears, large rats, and other aggressive fauna. [Source: Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md; Sessions/8. Rough Seas.md; Sessions/9. Emeliano is decead.md]
- Island lore ties to King Elard, mineral corruption, and fall into madness. [Source: Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md]
- Ruin/palace networks and mountain structures on the island lead directly into Shadow/Winter Regent arc content. [Source: Sessions/9-11]

## Relationships
- Crimson Consortium and Academy: expedition destination.
- The Winter Regent arc: staging ground for key discoveries.
