# Crimson Consortium

## Summary
Major commercial and political bloc with deep logistical reach. Intersects with both legitimate governance channels and suspicious covert links.

## Aliases
- Crimson

## Known Facts
- Dock strike records and seized documents repeatedly reference "The Cartel" and Consortium-linked pressure tactics. [Source: Sessions/3. Dock strikes.md]
- Jakobus Ket (logistics overseer) is a key Consortium-connected actor with intelligence access and expedition resources. [Source: Sessions/3. Dock strikes.md; Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md]
- Consortium sponsored/contracted Vaelor's Wound expedition with significant payout terms. [Source: Sessions/7. Looking for clues.md]
- Lady Crimson states strategic interest alignment with party against Golden Sails leadership. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]

## Relationships
- Jakobus Ket and Lady Crimson: central representatives.
- Golden Sails: economic rival and investigative target.
- Auric/Council politics: strong influence corridor.
