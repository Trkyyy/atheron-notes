# Lightbringers

## Summary
Historic and current-order force in Atheron/Corsava lore, repeatedly treated as a strategic obstacle by Shadow-aligned actors.

## Aliases
- Lanterns (possible colloquial or related reference; Unconfirmed exact equivalence)

## Known Facts
- Historical records credit Lightbringers with incinerating a prior Winter Regent-era target and securing remains in a vault. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md; Sessions/5.5. Meeting with the Crimson Cartel.md]
- Shadow-linked messages warn that a return of Lightbringers could disrupt long-term plans. [Source: Characers/The Winter Regent.md]
- A recovered note refers to "those Lanterns" causing trouble for Shadow operations (Unconfirmed if strictly synonymous with Lightbringers). [Source: Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]
- In a projected/apocalyptic view via telescope, Lightbringers HQ appears as a crater. [Source: Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]

## Relationships
- Shadow and Winter Regent operations: adversarial.
- Corsava civic order: historically aligned protective role.
