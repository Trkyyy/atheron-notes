# Corsava

## Summary
Primary political and urban center for campaign operations, featuring intense factional maneuvering among council governance, commercial powers, and covert threats tied to chaos escalation.

## Aliases
- Corsava (city and governing polity context used interchangeably in notes)

## Known Facts
- Frostviel Gala and Auric Council activities establish Corsava as a seat of power. [Source: Sessions/5. Frostviel Gala.md]
- Labor unrest, extortion conflicts, and powerbroker competition occur across wharf and gutter districts. [Source: Sessions/3. Dock strikes.md; Sessions/4. Gutter King.md]
- Post-island return conditions show curfew, mandatory IDs, increased disappearances, and public fear. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]
- Historical references tie Corsava's development to first fleet/lantern foundations and post-veil structures. [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]

## Relationships
