# Monarch (Five Thrones Framework)

## Summary
A major power structure implied to consist of five crown-linked seats/domains, referenced in symbols, notes, and throne architecture.

## Aliases
- Monarch
- The Five Greats (Lady Crimson phrasing)

## Known Facts
- Black coin recovered from Winter Regent body depicts a crown split into five pieces. [Source: Sessions/12. Getting bling.md; Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]
- Five themed thrones are discovered in Winter realm chambers, matching multi-domain iconography. [Source: Sessions/13. We killed the Winter Regent forever!!!!.md]
- Lady Crimson states "the 5 thrones are Monarch" and frames herself and Shadow as ancient monster/demigod-scale beings tied to this structure (Unconfirmed but direct testimony). [Source: Sessions/14. Back in Corsava.md]

## Relationships
- Shadow and Lady Crimson/Noir: likely direct members or former members.
- The Winter Regent mask line: appears to operationalize one throne/domain expression.
